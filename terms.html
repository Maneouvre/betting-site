Terms & Conditions.

These draft terms explain how the MatchMate prototype is intended to work. They are not legal advice and are not a licence to operate a gambling service. Have a qualified Kenyan gaming and privacy lawyer review them before any real-money launch.

Prototype only: MatchMate currently uses sample fixtures and manual/demo payment steps. Do not send money or rely on this site to hold, verify, or transfer funds.

1. Eligibility and local law

You must be at least 18 years old and meet any higher legal age that applies where you live. Do not use a real-money betting service in a place where it is prohibited. The operator must obtain all required gaming, consumer-protection, data-protection, tax, and payment approvals before enabling real-money play. This prototype does not confirm that any jurisdiction permits its use.

2. Accounts and registration

Register with your accurate name and a phone number you control. MatchMate assigns a registration account number for sign-in and support. Keep your password and account details private; the operator will not ask you to disclose your password. Do not create accounts for other people or use another person’s payment details without their permission.

3. Football matching

A football request is eligible to match only with another player choosing the same fixture, the exact same stake, and a different outcome. Identical picks (for example, both selecting Arsenal to win or both selecting Draw) are not paired. Players are paired before payment is requested. Unmatched requests are not payable. Betting closes 30 minutes before the listed kick-off time. The current fixture list is illustrative sample data, not a live schedule.

4. eFootball matches and rematches

eFootball requests match players at the same stake. Each round is one game. After the admin requests payment, players have five minutes to submit a payment reference. The admin must approve both references before each player may submit a room/invite code. Players are responsible for entering a fresh code for each new round and following the game’s rules.

After a round’s result is recorded, the same players may start a separate rematch even if the previous payout is still pending. A rematch has a new, equal stake, new payment references, fresh admin approvals, and new room codes. Stakes and payouts remain separate by round.

5. Payment references and manual approval

In a live round, each player must make their stake payment from the M-Pesa number registered to their own MatchMate account. Do not pay from another person’s number. The payment form records your sender name and reference and asks you to confirm the registered number, but this is not independent verification. The admin must compare the transaction sender name and number with your registered details and check the reference against the relevant Safaricom account before approving it.

eFootball players must submit their team name and team-strength rating as displayed in the game. These values are self-reported and are not independently verified. Neither player may submit or view room codes until the admin approves both players’ payment references. Once approved, each room code is available only to the two matched players and admin accounts.

In the prototype, entering an M-Pesa reference only records information for admin review. It does not initiate or verify a payment, and the website cannot block a transfer made outside it. A payment must not be requested until a matching player is found. Never submit a false reference or send funds based only on a demo screen.

WhatsApp and SMS controls open a prefilled message in your chosen app; they do not automatically send a message. No official Safaricom payment API is connected.

6. Results, payouts, and fees

Both eFootball players may submit one result report per round. Consistent reports identify an agreed result; conflicting reports are referred to an admin. Room codes and player reports are not independent verification from Konami. Football results are entered by an admin. The prototype cannot automatically read game results.

For a decided win, the gross matched pot is both equal stakes combined. The operator’s manager fee is 10% of that pot, rounded to the nearest whole Kenyan shilling. The supplied Send Money fee estimate is then deducted from the remaining amount to calculate the final amount shown to the winner. This estimate is not confirmed as Safaricom’s official 2026 tariff; Till/Buy Goods charges and transfer amounts outside the supplied bands are not estimated. Actual charges may differ. A tie is handled as a manual refund of both stakes without a manager fee.

Payouts and refunds are manual. A dashboard amount is an estimate until an admin confirms the result and marks the transfer or refund as sent. The displayed estimate is not a promise that funds have been received.

7. Fair play and disputes

Do not collude, manipulate results, submit false payment references or results, share someone else’s account, or interfere with another player’s game. The admin may hold a disputed match for review, cancel an invalid request, or request further information. Before launch, the operator must publish a clear complaint and appeal process with real contact details.

8. Personal information

The configured database stores account names, registration numbers, M-Pesa phone numbers, password hashes, bet details, payment references, room codes, result reports, and admin decisions. Admin accounts can see information needed to review matches and manual payments. Keep server access restricted, use HTTPS and secure backups, set a retention period, publish a privacy notice, and follow applicable data-protection law before production use. Do not enter sensitive or real payment data into an untrusted demo server.

9. Availability and third-party services

The prototype may be unavailable or contain errors. Sample fixtures, user-reported results, and fee estimates can be incomplete or inaccurate. MatchMate is not endorsed by Konami or Safaricom. Their apps, games, payment services, fees, and terms are controlled by those providers.

10. Changes and support

These draft terms are version 2026-10-20-demo-5. eFootball squad names and strengths are self-reported by players and are not verified against Konami. A production operator should show the applicable terms version and effective date, require renewed acceptance when material changes are made, and provide a working support contact before allowing real-money play. No support contact has been configured in this prototype.
